# Milestone 8 — Basket data-quality diagnostics

LifeBeta now explains whether a saved basket is ready for a trustworthy comparison before presenting an index as complete.

For every product it reports eligible observation count, number of distinct sources, first and latest eligible dates, current age, and explicit issues. Blocking issues are missing data, insufficient history, and staleness. A single source is disclosed as concentration risk without automatically blocking the calculation.

Future observations are excluded from historical quality reports. The overall coverage percentage is the share of basket products that meet the selected minimum-history and freshness requirements—not a statistical confidence claim.
